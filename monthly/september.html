❄ September 2026
What shipped

16 headline features shipped — the highlights.

⭐

Jellyfish check-in v1.9.20

Tag a swim with jellyfish and we'll ask how many you saw and which lane you were in, so the club can warn everyone else.

⭐

Your week, in the club insight v1.9.23

The insight of the week now adds a line about your own swimming &mdash; your first cold swim of the season, your coldest so far, or how your week compared. Worked out on your phone; nothing personal leaves it.

⭐

Air temperature fills itself in v1.9.21

Log a swim at Sandkaj and the air temperature and real feel fill in from the weather at the time you actually swam. Anything you type yourself stays.

⭐

Log a Swim, redesigned v1.9.24

The form now matches the rest of the app — clean cards, a headline water temperature, and everything on one screen.

⭐

Weather on every swim v1.9.24

Sandkaj swims get a tappable Weather stat: felt temperature, wind, gusts, rain and direction, measured at the swim's own time.
