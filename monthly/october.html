Water and swims

Each degree colder took about 0.5 min off a swim.

From 16.0° down to 11.0°: about 3 min and 91 m shorter, each swimmer compared with themselves. A small and not yet certain effect.

❄ October 2026
What shipped

14 headline features shipped — the highlights.

⭐

Log a swim, redesigned v2.0.2

The log form has a new layout: your note sits beside your photos, swim buddies show as faces you tap to add or remove, and an Add a swimmer sheet suggests who just swam nearby. Editing only asks before discarding if you've actually changed something.

⭐

Events redesigned v2.4.7

Events now look like swims, with a next up card and an easier way to create one.

⭐

Profile has season and all-time tabs v2.4.10

Your profile now splits into This season and All time tabs, with your longest streak and swims a tap away.

⭐

Next badges v2.5.9

Club totals now show the badge you're closest to, with a progress bar.

⭐

Shared first names show last initial v2.5.15

If another member shares your first name, your name now shows a last initial so people know who swam.
