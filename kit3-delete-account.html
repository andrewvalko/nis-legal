Delete your account — KIT3

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in KIT3 ("the App"), the photo-exchange app operated by Andrew Valko, and what happens to your data when you do.

Delete it in the App

  1. Open KIT3 and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion starts at once and cannot be undone.

If you can't use the App

Email awvalko+kit3@gmail.com from the email address your account uses and ask for it to be deleted. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted right away

  • Your account: email address, sign-in details, display name and avatar.
  • The photos and videos you sent.
  • Your connections ("KITs"). Each one ends for both people.
  • Your invite links, notification tokens and the nicknames you gave friends.
  • Feedback you sent us, with its screenshots.
  • Our record of your supporter subscription. We also ask RevenueCat, our subscription service provider, to delete theirs.

What is deleted within about 45 days

  • What was left inside the connections that ended: captions, weekly statuses, comments and reactions, yours and your friend's. Nobody can open them in the meantime.

What is kept

  • Technical server logs that may mention your account's ID. They expire within 30 days.
  • Nothing else.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through Google Play or the App Store. Cancel it in the store's subscription settings.
