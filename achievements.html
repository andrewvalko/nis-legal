Achievements

Every badge in NIS Tracker: 41 you earn yourself and 39 the club earns together. A badge appears on the swim that earns it, and yours collect on your profile.

What counts
  • Swims at Sandkaj. Swims at other places, and swims labelled pool or wetsuit, are in your log but earn no badges. Dips count like any other swim.
  • 1 October to 30 April. Swims from May to September earn nothing. The one exception is First Plunge, which your first Sandkaj swim earns any time of year.
  • Every season, a fresh start. Badges marked “Every season” start over on 1 October and can be earned again. Each one carries its season, like “25/26”.
  • Private swims count toward your own badges, but not toward the club’s.
