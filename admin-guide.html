A quick tour of the admin powers built into the NIS Tracker app. As an NIS board member, you're an admin by default.

Admin guide

You can moderate content and manage membership, plus a few extras. Everything here is built into the app — no separate dashboard, no web login.

What members hand over at sign-up

Worth knowing when you're approving someone, and when they ask you what the app knows about them.

Required

First name, last name, email and a password. That's the whole list — everything else can be skipped.

Optional

Home address, phone number and date of birth, on a second screen that says outright that it's all optional. Members can add, change or clear any of it later from Profile › Membership details.

Why optional  Apple doesn't allow gating account creation on details the app doesn't need to function. Approval is where completeness gets checked instead.

Where to find the optional details

Open any member's profile and you'll see a Membership details card with their email, address, phone and date of birth — anything they left blank shows as "Not provided".

Admins only  That card is invisible to everyone else. The pending-approval screen still shows only which fields are missing, not their contents.

Invite code

Not stored against the member. It only decides whether they land as approved or pending.

From anywhere in the app

Delete things

Long-press any comment, swim, or event to delete it. Useful if something's offensive or inappropriate.

Remove or lapse members

Open the member's profile — tap their name anywhere in the app — and you'll find Ban, Mark as Lapsed and Reinstate at the bottom. There's also a delete option in the club view at the bottom of the Club page.

Heads up  These live on profiles, not in the admin panel.

Admin control panel

You may have noticed a shield icon next to the notification bell in the upper right — that's how you get in. It's split into two groups: running the club, and running the app.

Club Management

Approve Pending Members

Sign-ups waiting for review, with a count next to the row when any are waiting. The app is in the public App Store, so take care to only approve people known to be in the club or joining it.

Shortcut  You'll also get a notification when someone signs up — tapping it takes you straight here.

Post Announcement

Push an announcement to the whole club. Sends a push notification and pins it at the top of the inbox. Could replace email blasts.

Modify Invite Code

Bypasses the pending step at sign-up. Pass it to new members during onboarding. Admins can rotate it if it leaks.

Modify Safety Terms

What users agree to at sign-up — release of liability and so on. Taken from the club's existing sign-up document; edit as you see fit.

Heads up  Every edit forces the whole club to re-consent.

Review Club Activity

Tracks requirements and nice-to-haves — swims with the club, swims under 5°, events created. Won't be useful out of season, but handy in October/November to see who met the bar. Have a look and flag anything that's unclear.

App Logistics

These features are used for managing the app itself, mostly for Andrew.

View App Feedback

Bug reports and suggestions members send from inside the app, with a count next to the row when any are unread. Mark each one actioned or declined once you've had a look.

View Flagged Content

If a user reports content as inappropriate, it shows up here, with a count next to the row. Apple requires it; hopefully it never gets used.

Configure Known Issue

Mostly for Andrew. Puts an alert at the top of a few pages when there's a bug affecting the app.
